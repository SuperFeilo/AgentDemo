# Skill: Verification (Reflection)

## When to use
Once — after evidence gathering, before composing the explanation.
The analyst re-derives its own headline numbers from the raw tool
results instead of trusting its working memory.

## Tool
None. Verification recomputes from the blackboard's stored tool results.

## Checks
1. **Number fidelity** — recompute cumulative change and peak deviation
   directly from the warehouse series; any restatement is corrected on
   the spot and recorded.
2. **Citation validity** — every citation candidate must still pass the
   weight/direction screen from `driver_analysis.md`; drop any that
   don't before they reach the explanation.

## Notes
- Numbers that survive re-derivation can be quoted with confidence;
  numbers that don't are exactly why this step exists.
- Corrections are always reported, never silent.
